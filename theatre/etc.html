ETC Eos

ETC Eos is a powerful family of lighting control consoles and software made by Electronic Theatre Controls (ETC) for theatre, television, and live events.

  • Controls Stage Lighting: Manages conventional lights, color-changing LEDs, and complex automated moving fixtures.
  • Uses Channel Syntax: Programmers type simple commands (like 1 [At] 50 [Enter]) into a command line to set fixture intensity and color.
  • Runs on Shared Software: Every device in the line uses the same core programming language and interface.

    Popular Hardware Models:

  • Eos Apex: The high-end flagship console built for large-scale productions and massive rigs.
  • Gio @5: A mid-sized, portable workhorse console with built-in touchscreens and motorized faders.
  • Ion Xe: A compact console popular for medium-sized venues and touring.
  • Element 2: A simpler, budget-friendly desk often used in school auditoriums.
  • ETCnomad: Software that turns a standard PC or Mac into a fully functioning Eos control or offline programming station.

    Generally speaking, they all run the "same" software (with the exception of the HOG consoles)



    A yap about ETC in schools

    You are more than welcome to skip this if your school doesn't deserve such good lighting control

    This section is basically a thing you can send to your school to explain it

    One of the main things that makes ETC (and specifically EOS/Nomad) great for schools is a feature called "Magic Sheets". Magic Sheets basically allow you to create your own custom frontend for the console - this could be as simple as 2 buttons with "lights on" and "lights off" for less technical people who just need the lights to work so they can see :)


    It can also be quite cost effective - £300 gets your school a usb activation key and a usb-dmx interface, so you can run it on a laptop (or mac I guess). Its quite flexible in the way you can attach touchsreen monitors, usb faders banks/keypads, and effectively make your own lighting console - expanding it if (or hopefully when) your budget does.
