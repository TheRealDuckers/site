Who I Am

Hey! I'm Duckers — a web developer and theatre tech engineer from the UK. I love building tools that make live productions smoother and more accessible.

I'm an active member of Hack Club and spend my time coding, singing, and creating cool projects. When I'm not coding, you'll find me working on lighting designs or writing blog posts about what I've been building.
